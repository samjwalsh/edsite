## What is a loss assessor?

A loss assessor is a claims professional appointed **by you**, the policyholder, to prepare and negotiate your insurance claim. That's different from a **loss adjuster**, who is appointed by the insurer.

## Why use one?

- We know what your policy covers and how to evidence every part of the loss.
- We deal with the insurer and their adjuster on your behalf.
- We coordinate contractors, surveyors and restoration companies.
