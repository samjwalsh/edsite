# About Us

## Claim Management Services

With over 20 years experience in handling all manner of insurance claims, we at CMS have the knowledge, skill and experience required to help you when making a claim. We cater for both Residential and Commercial claims and manage the experience from beginning to end, always looking after you – our client. By supplying a personal one-to-one service, we remove the stress and hassle that can accompany making a claim.

We at CMS are Loss Assessors, not Loss Adjusters.

## What is a Loss Assessor?

Loss Assessors, such as ourselves in CMS, work for you.

We look at your policy in detail and ensure that you are receiving what you are entitled to. We act as your experts, working with your best interests in mind.

## What is a Loss Adjuster?

Loss Adjusters work for, and are paid by, the Insurance Company.

They propose and negotiate as economical a settlement as possible on behalf of the Insurance Company.
