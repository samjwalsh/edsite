# Subsidence and Pyrite Claims

We are there to explain the implications of the various specialist reports and guide our clients through the claim process, setting out options and helping them to make decisions that are right for their circumstances.

We are able to help our clients to take practical steps to find alternate accommodation, seek their own independent reports, as well as repair and reinstate their buildings.

We have links with specialist companies so that whether your claim requires a contractor, or a surveyor, an engineer, or monitoring company, we are able to call upon the services of specialist companies that we have been working alongside for many years and we can trust.

Our aim is to obtain the maximum benefit for our clients from their Insurers, whilst removing the stress that a claim brings by guiding our client step-by-step through the claims process.
