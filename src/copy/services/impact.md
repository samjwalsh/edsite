# Impact Claims

We have links with specialist companies so that whether your claim requires a contractor, or a surveyor, an engineer, or restoration company, we are able to call upon the services of specialist companies that we have been working alongside for many years and we can trust.

In commercial settings we also deal with the business interruption, or loss of profit, aspects of the claim.

Our aim is to obtain the maximum benefit for our clients from their Insurers, whilst removing the stress that a claim brings, by guiding our client step-by-step through the claims process.
